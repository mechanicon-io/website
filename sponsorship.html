tiers for sponsoring

Mechanicon brings together creators, designers, vendors and enthusiasts from all over Europe. Pick the tier that fits you — a Commercial Exhibitor table for brands, or an Independent Designer Showcase spot for individual makers.

for registered companies & brands

Commercial Exhibitor

300 EUR*

What's included

  • Dedicated commercial exhibitor table
  • Opportunity to showcase products throughout the event
  • Company logo on Mechanicon.io sponsor page
  • Logo on rotation on the main stage throughout the event
  • Instagram sponsor announcement before and during the event
  • Opportunity to include products in the main raffle
  • Brand mentioned during raffle and stage announcements
  • Drink vouchers for your team
  • Support from the Mechanicon team during setup and the event
  • Networking opportunities with creators, designers and industry partners
  • Priority placement in the commercial area
  • Earlier setup for Commercial Partners only
  • Opportunity for presentations in the GMK room

Rules

  • Price does not include VAT(*). An invoice will be provided by Mechanicon e.V.
  • During the event your table is set and may not be changed, unless discussed otherwise with the Mechanicon team.
  • All displays, banners and promotional materials must remain within the allocated booth space.
  • Sale of products is not allowed. This is a strict rule we have to follow as a non-protit org (Verein).
  • Commercial Partner tier is intended for registered companies, retailers, manufacturers, distributors and commercial brands.
  • Subletting or sharing tables with another company is only permitted with prior approval from the organizers.
for independent creators

Independent Designer Showcase

50 EUR*

What's included

  • Affordable way to showcase your work
  • Dedicated Designer Showcase area
  • Display up to 2 products or projects
  • Special Designer badge
  • Featured on Mechanicon.io as a participating designer/community member
  • Community-focused area with visitors specifically interested in new projects
  • Dedicated designer information card describing your work, provided by Mechanicon
  • Excellent networking with vendors and manufacturers
  • Opportunity to hand out business cards or stickers

Rules

  • Commercial companies are not eligible.
  • No retail sales or commercial booths.
  • Independent creators only.
  • Your position will be set by the Mechanicon team.
  • Price does not include VAT(*). You may request an invoice from Mechanicon e.V.
  • You will have a set area on the table with limited space. Within this space you may put your branded material.

Perfect for

  • Keycap, keyboard designers
  • Cable makers
  • Switch lubing / keyboard building services
  • Artisan makers
  • PCB designers
